Wishing License
Permission to think creatively — to imagine, bend the rules, and take bold mental leaps without asking for approval.
  • Freedom to explore ideas that don't follow conventional logic.
  • Authority to experiment — even if it gets messy.
  • Space to express your unique vision, even if it breaks a few "rules."

Educated Out of Us
Most of us were taught to get the right answer — not to find new ones.
  • School rewarded correctness. Work rewarded efficiency.
  • Neither left much room for what if or I wonder.
  • Creative thinking was educated out of us.
  • A Wishing License changes that.
WISHING LICENSE
🌈 DREAM PHASE · CREATE · WISHING LICENSE
No Meetings Principle
Creative thinking requires protected space — free from judgment, interruption, and practical objections.
  • Keep your dreaming separate from your deciding.
  • The creative mind and the critical mind are both valuable — just not at the same time.
  • Protect the generative phase. No meetings during the dream.

Flip the Friction
What you don't want is a map to what you do.
  • When you don't know what you want, start with what you don't want.
  • Identify the friction — then flip it.
  • Every frustration points toward a hidden wish.
FRICTION
🌈 DREAM PHASE · CREATE · FRICTION
Combustion Point
An Emotional Issue. The moment when too much becomes too much.
  • The Accumulation — You've been holding it together. Then something small tips the balance.
  • The Shutdown — You stop responding. A door closes.
  • The Spiral — One thing goes wrong and suddenly everything is wrong.

🌈 DREAM PHASE · CREATE · FRICTION
Pain Point
A specific, everyday frustration that causes friction or stress in your life.
  • Financial — the pain of paying, budget strain, hard to save.
  • Productivity — never enough time, hate of the wait.
  • Emotional — the weight you carry quietly. Stress, connection, or the lack of it.

T2T
What is T2T?
T2T — Thoughts to Things — is a system for transforming imagination into reality.
Not a philosophy. Not something you have to believe in before it works.
A system. It works the first time you try it.
T2T
T2T Goals
May you find…
🔹 A clear Vision of what you really want.
🔹 The Pursuit of Excellence in everything you do.
🔹 A Beautiful Journey for everyone involved.
🔹 Every great invention started as a thought.
The light bulb… The airplane… The internet…
The vaccine… The symphony.
Every single one — thought first.
And every one of them began the same way.
