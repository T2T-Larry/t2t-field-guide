No Meetings Principle
Creative thinking requires protected space — free from judgment, interruption, and practical objections.
  • Keep your dreaming separate from your deciding.
  • The creative mind and the critical mind are both valuable — just not at the same time.
  • Protect the generative phase. No meetings during the dream.

Flip the Friction
What you don't want is a map to what you do.
  • When you don't know what you want, start with what you don't want.
  • Identify the friction — then flip it.
  • Every frustration points toward a hidden wish.
FRICTION
🌈 DREAM PHASE · CREATE · FRICTION
Combustion Point
An Emotional Issue. The moment when too much becomes too much.
  • The Accumulation — You've been holding it together. Then something small tips the balance.
  • The Shutdown — You stop responding. A door closes.
  • The Spiral — One thing goes wrong and suddenly everything is wrong.

Pain Point
A specific, everyday frustration that causes friction or stress in your life.
  • Financial — the pain of paying, budget strain, hard to save.
  • Productivity — never enough time, hate of the wait.
  • Emotional — the weight you carry quietly. Stress, connection, or the lack of it.
