CREATIVE SPARK · TRIVIA
1121 · Two Ways of Thinking
Your brain has two gears.
Creative thinking generates without judgment. Analytical thinking evaluates what was generated. Both are essential — but they cannot run at the same time.
A creative session has one rule: generation only. The moment you evaluate an idea — even silently — the creative gear disengages. Walt Disney called these two modes the Dreamer and the Critic. He never let them in the same room.
Right now, we only want the Dreamer.
CREATIVE SPARK · TRIVIA
1122 · Rules of Creative Thinking
Creative thinking has only one rule.
No criticism. Not of others' ideas. Not of your own. Not even a raised eyebrow. Not even the subtle internal wince that says that's a dumb idea.
Everything gets a green light in the creative session. Quantity over quality. Wild over safe. The Sea doesn't care how good an idea is — it only cares that you caught it before it vanished.
Evaluation comes later. Much later. That's SHAPE's job — not CREATE's.

T2T
What is T2T?
T2T — Thoughts to Things — is a system for transforming imagination into reality. Not a philosophy. Not something you have to believe in before it works. A system. It works the first time you try it.
