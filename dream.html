🌈 The Dream Phase

What is the Dream Phase?

The Dream Phase is where possibility lives before it becomes a plan. Here you're not solving problems yet — you're opening space. You give yourself permission to imagine without limits, generate ideas freely, and discover what genuinely excites you.

What happens in this phase?

You'll move through three creative territories: CREATE — where ideas flow freely and you build your Sea of Ideas. SHAPE — where you cluster, sort, and find what resonates. SHARE — where your dream takes form as a vision others can see.

Why does it matter?

Most great inventions began as a thought someone wasn't sure they were allowed to have. The Dream Phase gives you that permission — officially, and in writing.
