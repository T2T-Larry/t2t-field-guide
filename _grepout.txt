In this order.\n1. **Simplicity** — One point per page. One question. One moment. Nothing more.\n2. **Magic & Memorable** — The design test: *is this magic? Will they remember it?*\n3. **Content + Function first** — Get it right and working before making it pretty or fast.\n4. **The path can be offered. It cannot be imposed.**\n5. **River principle** — The path flows naturally. Effort is only required to climb out. If a step creates resistance, the step is wrong — not the person.\n6. **Two-response test** — Every step must earn \"Yes!\" and \"Tell me more.\" If it doesn't, redesign the step.\n7. **On Stage test** — Could a traveler hear this word without breaking the spell? If not, it stays backstage.\n8. **MAP + Journal as navigation pair** — MAP = where you could go. Journal = where you have been. A traveler can look at their Journal and see not just what they have done, but what they have not touched yet. That absence is itself a signal.\n9. **Claude as SME** — When a traveler hits a question they cannot answer, they do not get stuck. Research engine and thinking partner simultaneously.\n10. **AI organizes, the traveler decides.** AI may sort, group, or surface patterns — never judge, choose, or decide on the traveler's behalf. Anything that asks \"does this matter to me\" or \"which do I want most\" belongs to the traveler alone.\n## 🎯 Purpose / Goal / Objective / Values Taxonomy — Locked July 5, 2026\nApplies to any Topic/project/storyboard, at any fractal level.\n- **Values** — the how. Qualities insisted on while pursuing anything (honesty, care, craft). Don't point anywhere; they constrain movement, not direction.\n- **Purpose** — the why. A direction, not a destination — never completable, you live inside it rather than arriving at it. **Purpose is the ongoing criteria for judging the success or failure of the project/storyboard** — not just an entry-gate filter, a yardstick applied throughout and at the end too. Test: ask \"why?\" of it — if there's no further answer, that's the bottom of the stack; that's Purpose.\n- **Goal** — a describable end-state in service of the Purpose. No deadline required, but you can picture \"arrived.\" Test: ask \"why?\" of it — a Goal always has an answer, and that answer is the Purpose (or a Goal further up the chain).\n- **Objective** — a Goal made SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound).\n- **Purpose gut-check (traveler-facing framing):** \"Why are we doing this? If there's no good answer, don't do it.\" Functions as a gate before investing in a Topic, and (per the criteria-for-success framing above) as a judge applied again once the Topic is built out.\n- **Purpose placement — Locked July 5, 2026:** Purpose renders as the **first panel in a Storyboard's flow**, not a corner utility button — matches Feel First, Apply Later (the traveler feels the why before anything else, and every following card is read in light of it).
