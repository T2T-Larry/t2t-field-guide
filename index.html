Every great invention started as a thought.
The light bulb… The airplane… The internet…
The vaccine… The symphony.
Every single one — thought first.
And every one of them began the same way.
Someone answered one question:
THOUGHT
Thoughts happen.
We are constantly thinking.
Thoughts occur both consciously
and subconsciously.
1 of 3
THOUGHT
Conscious thoughts make up only about
5%
of our total thoughts.
Even at 5%, humans have more than
60,000
conscious thoughts per day.
2 of 3
THOUGHT
But, don't worry.
Scientists tell us that 98 percent of those 60,000 thoughts are repeats from the day before.
The other 2% are new thoughts.
Thoughts with imagination.
Walt Disney was fired from his first newspaper job for “lacking imagination and having no original ideas.”
3 of 3
What do you want?
"Most people don't know what they want, but they're pretty sure they haven't got it."
— Alfred E. Neuman
Do you know what you want?
That's completely fine.
Most people don't — not yet.
It is time to dream a little...
That's a great place to start.
If you sort of know what you want, let's see if we can capture it.
What if there were no limitations?
