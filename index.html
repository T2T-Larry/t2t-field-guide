About This Page
What is T2T?
T2T — Thoughts to Things — is a system for transforming imagination into reality.
Not a philosophy. Not something you have to believe in before it works. A system.
It works the first time you try it.
T2T Goals
May you find…
A clear Vision of something you want to create.
Inspiration
The Excellence to make it real.
Mastery
A Beautiful Journey along the way.
Joy in the process

Meet the authors
A revolution that started with a conversation.
Larry's grandfather invented OASIS — the green foam inside every flower arrangement you've ever seen. Something small. Something nobody thinks about. Something that just works. Larry grew up inside a family business built on a thought that became a thing.
He hired Bill as an accountant. That still makes him laugh. Bill turned out to be more creative than almost anyone he's ever met. Bill went on to work directly for Disney.
